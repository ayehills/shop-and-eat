How it fits together

Shop is your grocery run. Pantry tracks what's left after each meal you log. Eat is the daily log — tap foods as you eat them and watch the calorie ring and macro bars fill toward target. Add or remove foods on any day; custom foods pull from your pantry too.

Every day is identical on purpose — same menu, repeatable, easy to shop for. Everything saves on this device automatically.
